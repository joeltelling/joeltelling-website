How to play

Jump / fly / flip: Space ↑ W click or tap. Hold to keep jumping as a cube, to rise as the ship, to zig-zag as the wave. Tap to flip the ball or hop the UFO.

Shoot: X Shift right-click, or the FIRE button. The ship fires automatically. Shoot drones, orbiters and turrets (and their bullets!) for points and combos.

Orbs need a fresh click while you touch them. Pads launch you automatically. Portals change your vehicle or gravity. Collect the three π coins.

Practice mode: Z drops a checkpoint, Backspace removes it.   Esc pauses.
